HCP Anti-Deepfake Liveness & Licence Verification

Prototype for combining healthcare professional licence data with an experimental camera-based anti-deepfake liveness check (remote PPG). Designed as an additional security layer for regulators and insurers in cross-border telehealth to detect spoofed or synthetic video streams.
Note: This is not a certified medical device, identity verification system, or anti-fraud product.

Healthcare Professional
Anti-Deepfake Liveness & HR (Experimental)

Webcam-based remote PPG (rPPG) is used to estimate heart rate and to extract anti-deepfake indicators from multiple facial regions (forehead and cheeks). The algorithm computes a liveness score and flags weak or inconsistent physiological signals that may indicate spoofing, replay or deepfake video.

Status: Camera not started.

  • Face centered in video, well lit (avoid virtual backgrounds if possible).
  • Remain as still as possible during the 30s check to maximize signal quality.
  • Result is an experimental anti-deepfake signal and must be combined with standard identity and licence verification processes.
