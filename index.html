Encounter Liveness Check

Simple live camera check as an anti-deepfake safety layer.

Status: Idle

Use only within your organization’s security and data-protection policies; no guarantee of identity by itself.

What this app does

This app runs a short liveness sequence (head movement tasks) and shows a clear progress bar. It is a minimal testbed for your anti-deepfake concept and can be extended with more advanced checks or backend verification.

1. Purpose & Scope

This module provides a structured challenge–response liveness check for remote encounters. It is designed to protect all external stakeholders:

  • Payors (insurance, health funds)
  • Regulators (health authorities, data-protection bodies)
  • Administrators (hospitals, clinics, provider networks)
  • Executive Boards (governance, risk oversight)
  • Patient Advocates (fairness, integrity, patient safety)

Primary goal: reduce impersonation, replay attacks, and deepfake misuse in telehealth, claims, consent, and remote documentation workflows.

2. Position in Governance Architecture

This module sits between:

  • Identity assurance (ID documents, patient/provider records)
  • Operational workflow (EHR, CRM, claims, telehealth platforms)

Output is interpreted as: “Liveness completed; human-like response detected – requires human or automated review.”

3. Privacy & Data Protection

  • Runs 100% locally in the browser (demo implementation)
  • No data is sent, stored, profiled or transmitted by default
  • Can be deployed in GDPR/HIPAA-compliant environments
  • Any storage and logging is controlled by the implementing organization

4. Technical Characteristics

  • Uses getUserMedia for camera access
  • Behavioral prompts: look straight → turn left → turn right → nod
  • No biometric templates are created in this demo
  • Designed to be extendable with:
    • Face tracking and landmark analysis
    • Spoof detection (e.g. flat-screen patterns, glare)
    • Backend risk scoring and audit logging

5. Stakeholder Risk Controls

  • Not a full identity or fraud-prevention system
  • Reduces exposure to basic deepfake / replay risks
  • Supports fairer claims handling and telehealth integrity for payors
  • Supports governance, AI-risk and cyber-security reporting for boards
  • Protects patients from impersonation and misuse of their identity
  • Must be combined with:
    • ID / KYC verification
    • Contextual risk scoring (transaction size, device, geography)
    • Clear escalation routes for suspicious sessions

6. Integration Roadmap

  1. Pilot implementation with a defined user/patient/provider group
  2. Add a quantitative liveness score (0–100) with computer vision checks
  3. Log liveness events in secure backend systems (timestamp, device, score)
  4. Attach decision rules to score thresholds (e.g. score < 60 → manual review)
  5. Include this module in internal AI, digital-health, and risk documentation

User Help – Encounter Liveness Check

1. Purpose

Confirms that the camera is seeing a live human reacting in real time.

2. How to use

  1. Click “Start camera” and allow access.
  2. Position your face with good lighting.
  3. Click “Run liveness check”.
  4. Follow the instructions on the screen.
  5. Click “Stop camera” when finished.

3. Tips for better readings

  • Use a well-lit room; avoid bright light behind you.
  • Place laptop/tablet on a stable surface.
  • Make clear, visible head movements when prompted.

4. Data & privacy

All processing runs locally in your browser in this demo; no video is uploaded.

5. Disclaimer

This is a technical liveness layer. It must be combined with your own identity, KYC, and risk-management procedures. It is not a stand-alone guarantee against deepfakes or fraud.
