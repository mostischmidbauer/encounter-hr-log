What this app does

This app runs a short liveness sequence (head-movement tasks) and offers an experimental camera-based heart rate estimate using remote photoplethysmography (rPPG). A face detector, where supported, is used to track the face so the signal ROI follows the subject, with digital zoom to emphasize eye contact.

1. Purpose & Scope

This module provides a structured challenge–response liveness check for remote encounters and an optional experimental HR (rPPG) signal. It is designed to protect:

  • Payors (insurance, health funds)
  • Regulators (health authorities, data-protection bodies)
  • Administrators (hospitals, clinics, provider networks)
  • Executive Boards (governance, risk oversight)
  • Patient Advocates (fairness, integrity, patient safety)

Primary goal: reduce impersonation, replay attacks, and deepfake misuse in telehealth, claims, consent, and remote documentation workflows. HR (rPPG) is a secondary, optional signal for research and monitoring concepts.

2. Position in Governance Architecture

This module sits between:

  • Identity assurance (ID documents, patient/provider records)
  • Operational workflow (EHR, CRM, claims, telehealth platforms)

Output is interpreted as: “Liveness completed; human-like response detected – requires human or automated review.” HR output is an experimental signal, not a clinical vital sign.

3. Privacy & Data Protection

  • Runs 100% locally in the browser (demo implementation)
  • No data is sent, stored, profiled or transmitted by default
  • Can be deployed in GDPR/HIPAA-compliant environments
  • Any storage and logging is controlled by the implementing organization

4. Technical Characteristics

  • Uses getUserMedia for camera access
  • Liveness: scripted prompts (look straight → left → right → nod)
  • HR (rPPG): face-tracking where supported (Shape Detection API), with a small green-channel ROI on the upper face, plus simple frequency analysis to estimate the dominant heart-rate band.
  • No biometric templates are created in this demo

5. Stakeholder Risk Controls

  • Not a full identity, fraud-prevention, or medical monitoring system
  • Reduces exposure to basic deepfake / replay risks
  • HR (rPPG) is strictly experimental and must not be used for diagnosis or triage

6. Integration Roadmap

  3. Log liveness and HR events in secure backend systems (timestamp, device, score)
  4. Attach decision rules to thresholds (e.g. score < 60 → manual review)
  5. Include this module in internal AI, digital-health, and risk documentation

User Help – Encounter Liveness & HR (rPPG)

1. Purpose

Confirms that the camera is seeing a live human reacting in real time and optionally estimates heart rate from subtle color changes in the face (rPPG).

2. How to use

  1. Click “Start camera” and allow access.
  2. Position your face in the center, with good, even lighting.
  3. Click “Run liveness check” and follow instructions (optional).
  4. Click “Start HR (experimental)” and remain still for 10–20 seconds.
  5. Read the HR value and signal quality indicator.
  6. Click “Save local” and/or “Export JSON” to keep the log.
  7. Click “Stop HR” and “Stop camera” when finished.

3. Tips for better HR readings

  • Use a well-lit room with light in front of your face, not behind you.
  • Keep your head as still as possible (no talking, minimal movement).
  • Avoid strong color changes on the face (colored light, heavy filters).

4. Data & privacy

All processing runs locally in your browser in this demo; no video is uploaded.

5. Disclaimer

The HR feature is experimental and not a medical device. It must not be used to make clinical decisions or emergency assessments.
