User Manual – HCP Anti-Deepfake Liveness & Licence Check

1. Purpose

This tool helps document healthcare professional licence details and provides an additional camera-based anti-deepfake liveness check. It is an auxiliary security and integrity layer and must always be combined with official identity, licence and clinical verification processes.

2. Setup & Positioning

  • Use a laptop/desktop webcam where possible.
  • Sit 40–70 cm from the camera.
  • Center your face in the frame and use the Zoom slider so the face fills most of the preview.
  • Use soft, even light from in front of you. Avoid strong backlight or heavy shadows.

3. Running a Liveness Check

  • Click Start Camera and allow browser camera permission.
  • Adjust Zoom and Light / Sensitivity (Normal or Low-light).
  • Keep your head relatively still and look at the camera.
  • Click Run Liveness Check (~10s).
  • Stay still while the countdown runs; do not talk or move excessively.

4. Understanding the Results

  • Liveness score (0–100): higher means stronger evidence of live video.
  • HR: simple camera-based estimate in bpm (may be “–” if the signal is weak).
  • Signal quality: numerical indicator of how strong and usable the variation is.
  • Frame variance: raw measure of brightness variability; higher is generally better.

PASS (≈75–100): strong natural variation consistent with live presence. BORDERLINE (≈50–74): mixed or limited variation; repeat or verify with additional methods. SUSPICIOUS (<50): very low variation; treat as high fraud risk and use strong identity verification.

5. Saving & Exporting

  • Use Save Entry Locally to store licence + liveness + HR data in this browser.
  • Use Export CSV to generate a log file for compliance, audits or insurer/regulator review.
  • Use Delete All Local Entries to clear all stored data from this device.

6. Privacy & Limitations

  • All data stays locally in your browser unless you export CSV.
  • There is no server, no cloud, and no external transmission in this application.
  • The tool does not perform identity proofing, certified licence validation, or clinical decision-making.
  • It is intended as a non-medical, non-diagnostic security/support layer for telehealth, insurers and regulators.
